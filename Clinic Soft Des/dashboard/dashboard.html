ABOUT US

Who We Are

The University Clinic provides accessible, quality health care to every student. Our team of physicians, nurses, dentists and counselors is here to help you stay healthy and ready to learn.

Through this appointment system you can book a visit, check available schedules and manage your health appointments in one place, with no long queues and no paperwork.

BEFORE YOU VISIT

What to Bring

  • Your valid student ID
  • Your appointment confirmation (screenshot or on screen)
  • Previous medical records or prescriptions, if any
  • A list of medicines you are currently taking
GOOD TO KNOW

Clinic Guidelines

  • Arrive 10 minutes before your appointment time
  • Cancel or reschedule if you can no longer make it
  • Wear a mask if you have cough or cold symptoms
  • Keep the waiting area quiet and clean

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended for consultations, dental visits and counseling. First aid and emergencies are accepted as walk-ins during clinic hours.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Open "View My Appointments" from the Home page to cancel or change your booking. Please do so at least a day before your appointment.

How long does a medical certificate take?

Most certificates are ready after your consultation. Some requests may need review and can take one working day.

Is my health information private?

Yes. Your records are kept confidential and can only be accessed by authorized clinic staff.

What should I do in an emergency?

Go to the clinic right away during clinic hours, or call the campus emergency hotline. For life-threatening situations, call your local emergency number immediately.
