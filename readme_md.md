# 🏥 NUFV-CARES
**National University Fairview Clinic Appointment, Reservation, and Engagement System**

NUFV-CARES is a web-based clinic management platform designed to support appointment booking, scheduling, and calendar reservations at National University Fairview. It resolves the challenges of shared booking systems by managing appointment booking and capacity per service (Medical and Dental), automating real-time slot tracking, handling cancellations, and automatically closing appointments when daily capacities are reached.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
*   Organize appointment information for easier access and monitoring.
*   Reduce the time and effort needed for clinic staff to determine the total number of appointments.
*   Provide clinic staff with a real-time, updated view of patient appointments and their statuses.
*   Help clinic staff efficiently monitor cancelled appointments and track remaining available slots.

---

## ✨ Core Features
*   **Online Appointment Booking:** Book clinic appointments by selecting a preferred service, date, time slot, course/year level, and purpose.
*   **Service Selection & Capacity Management:** Choose between Medical or Dental consultations. Authorized staff can set and manage separate daily capacities for each service to prevent overbooking.
*   **Real-Time Dashboard Monitoring:** Clinic staff receive an updated dashboard showing newly created appointments without manual data processing.
*   **Calendar-Based Scheduling:** View clinic availability and select available dates and time slots based on configured scheduling intervals.
*   **Cancellation and Rescheduling:** Cancel or manage appointment changes, which automatically updates system availability.
*   **Automatic Booking Closure:** Automatically stops accepting appointments for a specific clinic service once its configured daily capacity is reached.
*   **Notifications and Messaging:** Appointment-related communication regarding confirmation and cancellations.
*   **RFID Integration:** Supports quick and secure user authentication via RFID card scanning.

---

## 👥 Target Users
*   **Students, Faculty, and Staff:** Book appointments, check open slots in real time, and receive notifications without falling in line.
*   **Clinic Staff and Front Desk:** Monitor new appointments, track availability, and eliminate manual Excel exporting and monitoring.
*   **Clinic Administrators:** Manage service capacities, system settings, and generate summary reports.

---

## ⚠️ Scope and Limitations
This system is intended specifically for NU Fairview Clinic operations. Please note the following limitations:
*   **No Medical Records:** The system does not store, manage, or process patient health or medical records.
*   **No Medical Advice:** The system does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or prescriptions, and does not replace professional consultation.
*   **No Emergency Channel:** Messaging is strictly limited to appointment-related communication and does not function as an emergency channel.
*   **No Payments:** The system does not process online medical payments or billing.
